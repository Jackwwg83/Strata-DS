# K15: hyper-connection mixes for a prefill sub-batch

CI-TARGETS: ds41_k15_hc_prefill k15_hc_prefill_test
CI-TEST: ./k15_hc_prefill_test
CI-FILES: src/ds41/kernels/k15_hc_prefill.cu src/ds41/kernels/k15/ ds41/tasks/K15.
CI-ISSUE: 8

## Why

Prefill (M3) runs the hyper-connection mixes twice per layer for every token. Today it calls K7 (decode: at most
8 tokens per call) in a loop: 512 launches per 4096-token sub-batch, 80 sub-batch calls per layer and pass at 32K.
Measured on an RTX 4090 (DS41_PF_PROFILE=1, 2026-10-06): 23% of a 32K prompt's GPU time (6.5 s of 27.9 s), 13% at
8K. The work itself is small: per token, 24 dot products over 20,480 values plus a 4x4 Sinkhorn.

## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k15_hc_prefill.hpp` (`hc_mixes_pre_rows`). Per token, exactly K7
(`ds41/tasks/K7.md`): `ops::hc_mixes` (rsqrt of the stream's mean square, 24 dot products with fn, the scales and
bases, sigmoid for pre and post, softmax and 20 Sinkhorn steps for comb) and `ops::hc_pre` with `pre_in`. Many
tokens per launch: fn (1.9 MB) can be read once per tile of tokens instead of once per 8.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k15_hc_prefill_test.cu`, fixed)

- Against `ops::hc_mixes` + `ops::hc_pre` per token, m = 1, 37, 300: every token's y relative L2 at most 1e-3; pre,
  post and comb relative L2 at most 1e-5 (as K7).
- Rule 7 graph check (m = 37). Workspace: `hc_mixes_pre_rows_workspace_bytes` decides its size.
- Ranking: `score_us` = median time of a 4096-token sub-batch. Baseline (K7 in a loop): see the first queue result.
