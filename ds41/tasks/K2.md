## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k2_fp8_gemm.hpp` (`fp8_block_gemm`). Prefill multiplies M tokens (up to
16384 per chunk) by DeepSeek's FP8 dense weights. Same math as K1 / `ops::fp8_linear`, for every row:

1. Activation: per row, blocks of 32 along K: `amax = max(|x|, 1e-4)`, `s = 2^ceil(log2(amax/448))`,
   `xq = fp8_e4m3(clamp(x/s, -448, 448))` (round to nearest even). The values `xq*s` are exact in BF16.
2. Weight: FP8 E4M3 `[N][K]` with one E8M0 scale (`2^(byte-127)`) per 32x32 block; `W*Sw` is exact in BF16.
3. `y = sum_k (xq*s)(W*Sw)` with FP32 accumulation; output BF16.

Because both operands are exact in BF16, a BF16 tensor-core GEMM (FP32 accumulate) on dequantized tiles is exact up
to summation order. FP8 tensor cores (sm_89+) are allowed only with an sm_86 fallback.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k2_fp8_gemm_test.cu`, fixed)

- 16 sampled rows per shape against `ops::fp8_linear`: relative L2 at most 2e-3. Shapes include M = 77 (tail).
- Ranking: `score_us` = total time over the 5 shapes. Also reported: `tflops_wq_b` (M=2048, N=32768, K=1280).

## Hints

Quantize the activations once into the workspace (as BF16 or FP8 plus scales), dequantize weight tiles in shared
memory, and use mma.sync / WMMA BF16 with FP32 accumulation. Baseline: one GEMV per row (extremely slow).
