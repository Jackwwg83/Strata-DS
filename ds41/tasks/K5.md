## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k5_indexer.hpp`. DeepSeek V4.1's two-level indexer picks, for each query,
the 512 compressed KV positions the attention reads. t (the number of compressed keys) grows with the context: about
16K at 32K tokens, 131K at 128K tokens.

`indexer_topk`: scores, optional candidate mask, top-k.
- `score_j = bf16( sum over 32 heads of bf16( relu(bf16(q_h . key_j)) * w_h ) )`: the dot product in FP32, rounded to
  BF16, relu, times the BF16 head weight and rounded to BF16, summed over heads in FP32 and rounded to BF16 once.
  Reference: `ops::indexer_scores` in `src/ds41/ops.cu` (restates `Indexer.forward` in `ds41/proto/ref/model.py`).
- Masked positions (cand[j] == 0) get -inf.
- Top-k: the k best, ties broken by lower position; output sorted ascending, plus `offset`.

`candidate_blocks`: `select_candidate_blocks` of model.py. Block score = max over each block of 8; the block with
position t-1 is always kept; keep the best `topk_blocks` blocks (ties: lower block); drop blocks scoring -inf.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k5_indexer_test.cu`, fixed)

- Scores equal the reference BF16 values for at least 99.9% of positions (the reference rounds after an FP32 sum in
  a fixed order; another order can flip a rounding, rarely).
- Top-k overlap with the reference at least 99.5%; output ascending. `candidate_blocks` exactly equal.
- Ranking: `score_us = us_t16k + us_t128k` (both with a candidate mask).

## Hints

- The scores are a [32 x 128] x [128 x t] GEMM followed by elementwise ops and a reduction over heads: tensor cores.
- Top-512 of 131K values: radix select or a two-pass histogram works well; avoid a full sort.
- Baseline: the M1 score kernel, then masking and top-k on the host (copies to and from the GPU).
