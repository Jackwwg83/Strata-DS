# K14: the indexer (scores, top-k, candidate blocks) for a prefill chunk

CI-TARGETS: ds41_k14_indexer_prefill k14_indexer_prefill_test
CI-TEST: ./k14_indexer_prefill_test
CI-FILES: src/ds41/kernels/k14_indexer_prefill.cu src/ds41/kernels/k14/ ds41/tasks/K14.
CI-ISSUE: 8

## Why

In prefill every token of a chunk is one indexer query: it scores all compressed keys it may see (causal), keeps the
best 512 for attention, and at the candidate layer (20) also selects candidate blocks that mask the later layers.
The baseline calls K5 once per query: 192 ms per layer for a 4096-token chunk at position 4096 on an RTX 3060 (8
indexer layers: 1.5 s per chunk; more at longer contexts, since every query reads every key). The scores are one
GEMM per head group: 4096 queries x 32 heads x 8192 keys x 128 dims is about 275 GFLOP, about 10-20 ms on the 3060.

## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k14_indexer_prefill.hpp` (`indexer_topk_prefill`). Query i sees the keys
`[0, t_i)`, `t_i = (pos0 + i + 1) / ratio`. Per query, exactly K5 (`ds41/tasks/K5.md`): scores as
`ops::indexer_scores` (bf16 rounding of each head's dot product, relu, times the head weight, bf16 rounding of the
product and of the sum), -inf where the query's candidate row is 0, the min(512, t_i) best positions (ties: higher
score, then lower position) ascending plus `offset`, -1 padded; with `cand_out`, K5's `candidate_blocks` on the
query's scores.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k14_indexer_prefill_test.cu`, fixed)

- 4 cases (a ratio-2 chunk from position 0, a candidate layer, a masked ratio-1 layer, a mid-context ratio-2 chunk):
  top-k overlap with the exact reference at least 99.5% (as K5), at most 0.1% of candidate-mask entries different,
  rows ascending and -1 padded, nothing written past t_i in the candidate rows.
- Rule 7 graph check (the candidate-layer case). Workspace: `indexer_topk_prefill_workspace_bytes` decides its size.
- Ranking: `score_us` = median time of a 4096-query chunk at position 4096, ratio 1, masked. Also reported:
  `us_m512`. Baseline on the RTX 3060 dev box: score_us 192,123; us_m512 20,890.

## Hints

Score many queries against a tile of keys at once (tensor cores, bf16 inputs, FP32 accumulation, then the bf16
rounding per head as the reference does), and select the top 512 per query with a radix or bucket select over the
bf16 score bits. Queries in a chunk see almost the same keys: only the last few keys differ (the causal limit).
