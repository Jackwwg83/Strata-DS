# K13: sparse attention for a prefill chunk

CI-TEST: ./k13_sparse_attn_prefill_test

CI-ISSUE: 8

## Why

Prefill (M3, `ds41/docs/m3-plan.html`) runs a chunk of up to 8192 prompt tokens through each layer at once. Every
token is one attention query over 640 KV rows (its 128-position sliding window plus the indexer's top 512 compressed
rows). The baseline calls K3 (decode attention) 8 queries at a time: 122 ms per layer for a 4096-token chunk on an
RTX 3060, 4.9 s per chunk over 40 layers. The math is about 344 GFLOP per layer at 4096 tokens (QK and PV, 64
heads, 640 rows, 512 dims): tensor cores can do it in about 10-20 ms on the 3060.

## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k13_sparse_attn_prefill.hpp` (`sparse_attn_prefill`). Per query, exactly
`ops::sparse_attn` (`src/ds41/ops.cu`), in its order: scores `q_h . kv_j * scale` in FP32 over the listed rows; the max
floored at -1e30; `p = exp(s - max)` rounded to bf16 for the PV numerator (unnormalized); the denominator is the
unrounded sum of `exp(s - max)` plus `exp(sink - max)`; FP32 accumulation; divide at the end; bf16 output.

The engine's layout (the test uses it): one KV buffer per layer and chunk, `[compressed rows][window rows for the
positions p0 - 127 .. p0 + m - 1]`. Query r lists its 128 window rows (oldest first, -1 before position 0: row
`n_comp + r + j`), then its top-k compressed rows (ascending, -1 padded). Neighbouring queries share 127 of their 128
window rows, and many compressed rows: a kernel that loads a row once for several queries (or several heads) saves
most of the traffic.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k13_sparse_attn_prefill_test.cu`, fixed)

- Relative L2 at most 3e-3 against `ops::sparse_attn`, over each case and for every single query (as K3), in 5 cases: a chunk at position 0,
  mid-context chunks of ratio 1 and 2, one query, n_idx 1024 and 128.
- Rule 7 graph check (m = 37).
- Ranking: `score_us` = median time of a 4096-query chunk (n_idx 640, position 4096, ratio 1). Also reported:
  `us_m512`. Baseline on the RTX 3060 dev box: score_us 120,956; us_m512 14,988 (after the adapter fix of 2026-10-06).
