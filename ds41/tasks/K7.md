## What it computes

Interface: `include/strata/ds41/kernels/k7_hc.hpp` (`hc_mixes_pre`). DeepSeek V4.1 carries the residual stream as 4
copies (hyper-connections). Every sub-block (attention, FFN; 80 per token) computes new mixing coefficients from the
4x5120 stream and collapses the stream with the previous coefficients. Reference: `ops::hc_mixes` and `ops::hc_pre`
in `src/ds41/ops.cu` (restating `Block.hc_mixes`, `hc_split_sinkhorn` and `Block.hc_pre`).

1. `r = rsqrt(mean(x^2 over all 20480 values) + 1e-20)`; `mix = (fn . x) * r` for 24 rows of fn (FP32 weights).
2. `pre = sigmoid(mix[0:4]*scale0 + base[0:4]) + 1e-6`; `post = 2*sigmoid(mix[4:8]*scale1 + base[4:8])`.
3. `comb = mix[8:24]*scale2 + base[8:24]` as 4x4; row softmax + 1e-6; then column-normalize; then 19 times
   (row-normalize, column-normalize), each with `+ 1e-6` in the denominator.
4. `y = bf16(sum_j pre_in[j] * x[j])`.

## Acceptance (`src/ds41/tests/k7_hc_test.cu`, fixed)

- Coefficients: relative L2 at most 1e-5; y: at most 1e-3. m in {1, 3, 8}.
- Ranking: `score_us = us_m1 + us_m8/8`.

## Hints

The 24 x 20480 FP32 weight (1.97 MB) dominates: read it once for all m tokens. One fused kernel can do the
reduction, the dot products, Sinkhorn (tiny) and the collapse. Baseline: 4 kernel launches per token.
